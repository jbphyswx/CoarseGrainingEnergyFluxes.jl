Filtering

The filter API, plans, mask strategies, engine selection and the process shares. The real-space engines are on Filtering engines, and the vector filter of the sphere on Filtering on the sphere.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.filter_field! — Method
filter_field!(out, field, grid, kernel, scale; mask_strategy = ZeroFill(), filter_plan = nothing,
              execution = AutoBackend(), method = RealSpace(), transform = AutoSpectralBackend())

Filter a field on a grid using kernel at characteristic full width scale (ℓ), writing the result to out (returned).

Keyword Arguments

  • mask_strategy::AbstractMaskStrategy=ZeroFill(): how masked cells and a bounded edge enter the filter. ZeroFill filters the field extended by zero over masked cells and past each bounded edge, normalized by the kernel's full mass over the lattice continued at its edge spacing, so filtering commutes with spatial derivatives, conserves the integral over all space, and defines the output at masked cells too; a uniform field falls toward a coast. Deformable normalizes over the active in-domain cells of each window and zeroes masked cells, so a constant is reproduced next to a boundary and the kernel changes shape there.
  • filter_plan::Union{Nothing,AbstractFilterPlan}=nothing: a prebuilt plan_filter result, reused across calls on the same grid, kernel and scale. When supplied, mask_strategy, execution, method and transform are ignored.
  • execution::AbstractExecutionBackend=AutoBackend(): execution backend (SerialBackend, ThreadedBackend, GPUBackend, …).
  • method, transform: as for plan_filter.

For spherical grids the longitude footprint wraps only when the grid is periodic (isperiodic); distances use the great-circle (Haversine) metric.

Examples

geom = CartesianGeometry()
grid = StructuredGrid(geom, 0.0:1000.0:99_000.0, 0.0:1000.0:99_000.0, mask)
out = zeros(100, 100)
filter_field!(out, field, grid, TopHatKernel(), 5000.0; mask_strategy = Deformable())

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.spectral_grid_plan — Method
spectral_grid_plan(spectral_backend, grid, kernel; mask_strategy, execution, batch, max_scale = 0)
    -> AbstractGridPlan or nothing

The half of a spectral plan a sweep shares: the transform objects themselves, the wavenumber grids, and the mask. The transfer function Ĝ(|k|, ℓ) and the Deformable renormalization depend on the filter scale, so a sweep builds this once and each scale keeps only those two. A bounded Cartesian direction is zero-padded so that kernel at every scale up to max_scale stays clear of the domain's periodic copies; a filter plan at a larger scale raises. max_scale = 0 pads to twice the direction's length. The transforms run on _library_threads(execution) threads.

Returns nothing for a backend with no grid plan; a sweep then builds a full plan per scale.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.plan_filter — Method
plan_filter(grid, kernel, scale; mask_strategy = ZeroFill(), execution = AutoBackend(),
            method = RealSpace(), transform = AutoSpectralBackend(), cache_strategy = AlwaysCache(),
            grid_plan = nothing, scratch = nothing, engine = AutoEngine(), fft_options = (;)) -> AbstractFilterPlan

A reusable filter plan for execution, applied by filter_apply!(out, field, plan): the real-space footprint, or under method = Spectral() the transform plan. engine names the real-space engine (AbstractEngine); AutoEngine() takes the one with the fewest operations (_select_engine). A spectral plan also takes batch = nb for a trailing batch axis and, over a spherical node set, nufft, the NUFFT library NUFSHT runs, and analysis (AbstractAnalysis); a keyword the method does not take raises an ArgumentError. grid_plan and scratch are the parts a sweep shares; see plan_filter_sweep. fft_options are the planner keywords of the FFTs the plan makes (the spectral FFT filter, the padded and zonal FFT engines), passed to the FFT library's plan_* as they stand, e.g. (; flags = FFTW.MEASURE) under FFTW.

A transform runs on 1 thread under SerialBackend() and on Threads.nthreads() under ThreadedBackend(), AutoBackend() and GPUBackend(), in the device's memory under the last. Under MPIBackend(inner) every rank holds the field and the output. A nonuniform transform (NUFFT, NUFSHT) gives each rank a disjoint share of the points: the analysis F_k = Σⱼ wⱼ cⱼ exp(−i k⋅xⱼ) is a sum over points, so the ranks' partial sums add to it exactly, and each rank evaluates the filtered series at its own points. An FFT or FastSphericalHarmonics transform runs whole on every rank. Under DistributedBackend(inner) the worker processes share the transform, a nonuniform one by blocks of points and an FFT by blocks of columns and then of rows, each at inner's thread count; a FastSphericalHarmonics transform runs in the calling process.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering._nufft_sum_ops — Method
_nufft_sum_ops(grid, kernel, scale, cutoff) -> (transform, entries, build)

The operations of one apply over Cartesian points by the nonuniform-FFT sum, and the neighbour list's entries (one operation each per apply) and the operations of its build. The sum is two transforms of M·w^D spreading and 2.5 N log₂ N over the twice-oversampled box of n_d = 2⌊K L_d/2π⌋ + 1 modes, w the spreading width ⌈−log₁₀ tol⌉ + 1 and L_d the box of _KernelBox; building its plan is one such apply, the window masses. The list holds each point's neighbours at the mean density, V_D r^D · M/ΣA, and is built from the point bins (_binned_csr) in two passes over the points of the bins within reach, D + 1 operations each (a squared distance and its test), and a weight and a store per entry.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.plan_filter — Method
plan_filter(grid, kernel, scale; mask_strategy = ZeroFill(), execution = AutoBackend(),
            method = _default_method(grid), transform = AutoSpectralBackend(), kwargs...)

The plan for a grid with no rectilinear or curvilinear engine of its own; method defaults to _default_method. In real space a flat-cell grid (see _is_flat_cell) has the node CSR engine over its own ball query, and any other grid raises. Under method = Spectral() the grid is filtered over its cells by the nonuniform transform of its geometry: NUFFT for a Cartesian one, NUFSHT for a spherical one.

RealSpace() applies the kernel with its compact support. A transform is exact for a band-limited field and its per-apply cost is independent of the filter scale; its support is global.

One plan_filter per scale in scales, all sharing one grid-determined half of the engine and one per-apply scratch: the scales' plans whose buffers have the same shapes hold one set of them. For the prefix-sum top-hat engine the shared half is the measure prefix scans, the ordered axis-1 coordinates and the mask scan. method = nothing takes the grid's default method. scale_rates = ScaleRates() also builds each real-space plan's scale_rate, which a sweep's exact filtering spectrum reads, so a family reused across calls builds them once; ScaleCurvatures() builds their second derivatives too, which the spectral slopes read.

Forwards every other plan_filter keyword.

for (s, plan) in zip(scales, family)
    filter_apply!(out, field, plan)

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.Deformable — Type
Deformable <: AbstractMaskStrategy

Masked cells and the exterior are excluded from both numerator and denominator, so the kernel is renormalized over the active cells in its window ("deformable kernel"), and a masked cell is zero. A constant is reproduced exactly next to a boundary, but the kernel changes shape there, so filtering neither commutes with derivatives nor conserves the domain integral.

source
CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.RealSpace — Type
RealSpace <: AbstractFilterMethod

The physical-space convolution with the compact kernel, on any grid, mask and geometry. The grid and kernel decide the engine that evaluates its sum. On a host backend that includes a transform of the same sampled kernel where the grid allows one: the FFT on a uniform Cartesian grid (circular along periodic axes, zero-padded along bounded ones) and the transform along longitude on a sphere of uniform longitude, which agree with the direct sum to round-off. Spectral, which multiplies by the kernel's transfer function, is a different discretization.

source
CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.Spectral — Type
Spectral <: AbstractFilterMethod

Transform-space filtering (kernel applied as a multiply on the transformed field). Requires a spectral extension and a compatible grid (e.g. using FFTW for a uniform Cartesian grid, periodic or bounded).

source
CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.ZeroFill — Type
ZeroFill <: AbstractMaskStrategy

Masked cells and the domain's exterior are zero-velocity water (Aluie et al. 2018; Storer et al. 2022): they contribute nothing to the numerator, and the kernel keeps its full mass, the cells past a bounded edge included. The kernel is then the same everywhere, so filtering commutes with derivatives and conserves the integral over all space, which on a periodic grid is the grid (Aluie 2019; Grooms et al. 2021, eq. 7).

The filtered field is defined at every cell, a masked one too: within the kernel's reach of an active cell it is nonzero over land.
