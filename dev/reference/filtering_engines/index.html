CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.FilterFootprint — Type
FilterFootprint{T,BT,MT,SC,MS}

Precomputed convolution footprint for a structured grid + kernel + scale whose axis 1 is uniform. The in-support neighbour offsets and their geometric weights w = kernel_weight(distance) * cell_area are held in a BandTable, grouped into axis-2 (y) bands. A Cartesian grid uniform in both directions is translation-invariant, a single band; otherwise the footprint is invariant along axis 1 only, one band per row.

The offsets and weights depend on the geometry alone. invden, the reciprocal window mass, also depends on the mask, the mask strategy and where a domain edge cuts the window; _banded_build_invden accumulates it at plan build.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering._banded_build_invden — Method
_banded_build_invden(grid, taps, nbands, strategy, periodic_x, periodic_y, exterior) -> invden

The reciprocal window mass, accumulated once per plan by running _banded_row_accumulate! over a mass field:

  • ZeroFill counts every in-support tap whether or not its source cell is active, so its mass field is 1 everywhere, and adds exterior, the mass past a bounded edge: the kernel's full mass. An exterior vector is that full mass per row, and is the denominator itself.
  • Deformable drops inactive cells from both sums, so its mass field is the mask, and an inactive target gets zero.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering._banded_lon_offsets — Method
_banded_lon_offsets(dl, Nx, dλ, periodic) -> NTuple{3,UnitRange{Int}}

The longitude offsets a latitude row's taps are drawn from, for a window of dl steps of dλ. A ring contributes each column once: -dl:dl capped at one turn, the antipodal column of an even ring taken from one side. A regional box takes the near offsets |Δi| ≤ dl and the far ones that come back within the window across its seam, |Δi|·|dλ| ≥ 2π − dl·|dλ|, up to Nx − 1; longitude identifies, so the distance between two columns is taken the short way round.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering._banded_unit_mass! — Method
_banded_unit_mass!(den, taps::BandTable, nbands, periodic_x, periodic_y) -> den

_banded_row_accumulate! over a mass field of ones, into a zeroed den, from the runs' weights alone. On a periodic axis 1 every target of a row takes every tap, so the row is one sum, added in the order the accumulation adds it. On a bounded axis 1 a target takes the part of each run whose sources stay on the row, a difference of the run's prefix sums. Cost O(taps) per row on a periodic axis 1 and O(runs·Nx) on a bounded one.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.prepare_row_apply! — Method
prepare_row_apply!(fp, field, grid) -> nothing

Whole-grid work an engine needs done once before any of its per-row applies run: the banded engine materializes its mask · field source, one slot per field for the batch form; the other engines need nothing. CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.BinnedEdgeCells — Type
BinnedEdgeCells{T,VV,C}

A curvilinear mesh's cells under a Kernels.CellOverlap top-hat of radius R, for the binned engine: each point's four cell vertices in bin order (vx, vy, at the point's own image), and the squared radii rin2 = (R − ρ)² within which a cell lies wholly inside the disk and rout2 = (R + ρ)² beyond which it lies wholly outside, ρ the farthest any vertex lies from its cell's centre. A cell between weighs its fraction inside; cache holds those fractions per target in bin order, (ptr, src, frac) with src a point in bin order, or is nothing where an apply computes them.

BinnedPoints{D,T}

A Cartesian point set binned on a lattice of nb boxes of widths h from lo, axis 1 fastest: bin b's points are order[ptr[b]:ptr[b+1]-1], and x, measure and mask hold the points in that order. Along a periodic direction the lattice tiles the period, and x is reduced into [lo, lo + period).

BinnedTopHatPlan{D,T}

The top-hat of radius rad over a BinnedPoints set: per offset lines[q] of a line of bins across axes 2…D, the half-widths win[q] of its run of bins inside the ball for any target in the bin (-1 for none) and wout[q] of the bins that may reach it; the per-apply buffers, one (values, scan, sums) triple per field in flight (the weighted values in bin order, the compensated scan of the bin totals along axis 1, and each target's window sum in bin order), added on demand by a batched apply; and invden, the reciprocal window mass under strategy per point in the grid's order, zero where the window is empty or, under Deformable, the target inactive. bound is whether the two strategies' denominators differ, which they do on a masked grid. edge is nothing where each point counts by the centre test (a node set, or a mesh under Kernels.CellCentre), the BinnedEdgeCells of a mesh under CellOverlap.

Along a uniform direction 1 a target's cells and weights are its row and level's shifted along it: a periodic ring keeps one list per (row, level) about column 1, an NDRingCache; a bounded axis one table of offsets per (row, level), from the folds about its two end columns, each target reading the offsets that stay on the grid. CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.SeparableFootprint — Type
SeparableFootprint{T}

Per-axis kernel weight tables (gx, gy, read through _sepw), the denominator, and the scratch buffers of the two-pass separable convolution.

The denominator is invrenorm for Deformable on a masked grid: the reciprocal kernel mass over active cells, the same two-pass convolution applied to the mask. Otherwise it is the outer product of Nx_profile and Ny_profile: ZeroFill counts the offsets in the domain and past a bounded edge, Deformable on a fully active grid only those in the domain. Whether an offset lies in the domain depends on i and j separately, so the profiles are rank-1.

Each axis is truncated at rad on its own, so the window is a square. For the Gaussian it differs from the disk-truncated engines by weights below the truncation tolerance of kernel_radius.

The separable engine's two pass buffers: the masked input, and the intermediate the row pass writes and the column pass reads.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering._separable_axis_weights — Method
_separable_axis_weights(spacing, x, lim, periodic, period, kernel, scale, wfac) -> AbstractVecOrMat

A separable kernel's per-axis weight table, Kernels.profile_cell_average over the stencil, in the layout _sepw reads, for a direction of spacing trait spacing (Grids.spacing_trait, a periodic direction's wrap included). Serves the Gaussian and Kernels.HighOrderKernel.

Uniform direction: the displacement is ddi·Δ at every position, so one vector serves them all. Otherwise an n × (2·lim+1) table, one stencil per position, each weight times the neighbour's width.

lim comes from the smallest gap on the axis, so on a stretched axis a coarse region's stencil has slots past rad, holding the kernel's negligible or zero tail. A periodic displacement includes the image shift, so each image of a cell counts at its own distance.
