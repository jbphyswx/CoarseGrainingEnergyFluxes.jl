CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.AbstractSpinTransforms — Type
AbstractSpinTransforms

Spin-weighted spherical harmonic transforms of spin 0, 1 and 2 over every cell of a spherical grid, built by spin_transforms, in the grid's element type. A subtype defines

  • spin_field(tr): a zeroed complex field array of the kind they read and write;
  • spin_field_size(tr): its size;
  • spin_position(tr): a callable taking a cell's linear index to its position in that array;
  • spin_coefficients(tr): a zeroed coefficient array;
  • spin_transfer_buffer(tr): a zeroed host vector of one entry per degree a coefficient array carries, from 0, for the transfer Ĝ;
  • spin_analyze!(C, F, tr, s): the coefficients of the spin-s field F into C;
  • spin_synthesize!(F, C, W, Ĝ, tr, s): into F, the spin-s field whose degree-n coefficients are Ĝ[n+1] times those of C, with W a coefficient array to scale in. C is left as it is.

and may define spin_host(tr), whether its arrays are host memory, true unless it says otherwise; a device workspace stages its fields through host copies for transforms that run there.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.CartesianComponents — Type
CartesianComponents <: AbstractVectorFilter

The scalar filter of each ambient Cartesian component of a vector, and of each Cartesian component of a tensor, rotated back to the local frame (Aluie 2019, eq. 41). On the surface of a sphere it equals GeneralizedConvolution for a tangent field with no divergence, whose Cartesian components of degree n are spherical harmonics of degree n; the components of a divergent field's degree-n part carry degrees n ± 1, which it filters with Ĝ(n ± 1), and it gives a tangent field a radial component, which a tangent diagnostic drops. It is how a vector is filtered under Deformable, which is no convolution, and on a spheroid.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.CompensatedScan — Type
CompensatedScan{T,A}

A prefix scan held as two tables: hi, the floating-point running sum, and lo, the running sum of the exact rounding error of each addition, so entry k is hi[k] + lo[k] to about twice T's precision (Ogita, Rump & Oishi, SIAM J. CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.Deformable — Type
Deformable <: AbstractMaskStrategy

Masked cells and the exterior are excluded from both numerator and denominator, so the kernel is renormalized over the active cells in its window ("deformable kernel"), and a masked cell is zero. A constant is reproduced exactly next to a boundary, but the kernel changes shape there, so filtering neither commutes with derivatives nor conserves the domain integral.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.DirectSpinTransforms — Type
DirectSpinTransforms(grid; lmax = nothing, execution = SerialBackend())

The spin-weighted transforms of GeneralizedConvolution by direct summation over the cells of a spherical grid, for any spin |s| ≤ 2, in the grid's element type and in the memory of the local backend execution, whose loops run them. With ₛY_ℓm(θ, φ) = N_ℓ d^ℓ_{m,−s}(θ) e^{imφ}, N_ℓ = √((2ℓ+1)/4π) and θ the colatitude, the analysis is the quadrature C_ℓm = Σ_k w_k F_k conj(ₛY_ℓm(x_k)) with w_k the cell's measure over R², one work item per order m, and the synthesis is F_k = Σ_ℓm Ĝ(ℓ) C_ℓm ₛY_ℓm(x_k), one work item per cell. The coefficients are C[ℓ+1, m+lmax+1].

The Wigner d^ℓ_{m,−s} run up the three-term recurrence in ℓ from ℓ₀ = max(|m|, |s|), where d is a product of powers of cos(θ/2) and sin(θ/2) built order by order, of size about sin^|m| θ. A value below 2^−B, B half the element type's largest binary exponent, is carried as a significand and its own exponent, so an order whose seed lies below the floating-point range keeps the values it reaches at higher degree. A transform costs O(M·lmax²) over M cells and allocates nothing. lmax = nothing takes spin_band_limit.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.FilterFootprint — Type
FilterFootprint{T,BT,MT,SC,MS}

Precomputed convolution footprint for a structured grid + kernel + scale whose axis 1 is uniform. The in-support neighbour offsets and their geometric weights w = kernel_weight(distance) * cell_area are held in a BandTable, grouped into axis-2 (y) bands. A Cartesian grid uniform in both directions is translation-invariant, a single band; otherwise the footprint is invariant along axis 1 only, one band per row.

The offsets and weights depend on the geometry alone. invden, the reciprocal window mass, also depends on the mask, the mask strategy and where a domain edge cuts the window; _banded_build_invden accumulates it at plan build.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.GeneralizedConvolution — Type
GeneralizedConvolution(; lmax = nothing) <: AbstractVectorFilter

The convolution of vectors and rank-2 tensors on a sphere of Aluie (2019, eqs. 58 and 121): every degree-n vector or tensor spherical harmonic is multiplied by the kernel's Ĝ(n) (Kernels.spectral_transfer_degrees!), so filtering commutes with the surface gradient, divergence and curl (Propositions 1–3). It runs as spin-weighted transforms of the field extended by zero over the sphere (spin_transforms): FastSphericalHarmonics' on a Float64 ClenshawCurtisSampling grid of 2N − 1 by N, NUFSHT's on every other spherical grid when a NUFFT library is loaded to run it, and otherwise the direct sums, RingSpinTransforms on a latitude–longitude StructuredGrid and DirectSpinTransforms elsewhere. The theory page derives the spin-weighted form.

lmax is the highest degree the NUFSHT and direct transforms carry, nothing the grid's spin_band_limit. FastSphericalHarmonics transforms at its grid's own band limit, so a given lmax selects another transform under transform = AutoSpectralBackend().

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.NUFFTSumFootprint — Type
NUFFTSumFootprint

The real-space filter over a Cartesian grid's cells as points, Σⱼ G(|x − xⱼ|) Aⱼ fⱼ / Σⱼ G(|x − xⱼ|) Aⱼ over the cells the strategy counts, its sums taken by nonuniform FFT. On a box whose bounded directions are padded by the kernel's radius, so that every image of a point lies past the radius from every other,

Σⱼ G(x − xⱼ) Aⱼ cⱼ = Σ_k Ĝ(k) e^{ik⋅x} Σⱼ (Aⱼ / ∏L) cⱼ e^{−ik⋅xⱼ}

(Potts & Steidl 2003), with |k| up to Kernels.transfer_cutoff. It is the node CSR engine's sum with the kernel continued past its truncation radius, where it is below Kernels.GAUSSIAN_TRUNCATION_TOL of its peak, and the transforms run at that tolerance.

invden is the reciprocal of the denominator, zero where it vanishes or, under Deformable, at an inactive node. bound is whether the two strategies' denominators differ, which they do on a masked grid.

References

  • Potts & Steidl (2003), SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 24(6), "Fast summation at nonequispaced knots by NFFTs".

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.RealSpace — Type
RealSpace <: AbstractFilterMethod

The physical-space convolution with the compact kernel, on any grid, mask and geometry. The grid and kernel decide the engine that evaluates its sum. On a host backend that includes a transform of the same sampled kernel where the grid allows one: the FFT on a uniform Cartesian grid (circular along periodic axes, zero-padded along bounded ones) and the transform along longitude on a sphere of uniform longitude, which agree with the direct sum to round-off. Spectral, which multiplies by the kernel's transfer function, is a different discretization.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.RingSpinTransforms — Type
RingSpinTransforms(grid; lmax = nothing, execution = SerialBackend())

The sums of DirectSpinTransforms on a two-dimensional latitude–longitude grid, whose rows share a colatitude. The analysis is C_ℓm = Σ_j N_ℓ d^ℓ_{m,−s}(θ_j) Σ_i w_ij F_ij e^{−imφ_i}: the sums along every row are taken at once by the grid's DenseLongitudes, or with FFTW loaded on a whole ring of uniform longitude by one FFT per row, and the Wigner recurrence runs once per row and order, one work item per order, each degree a pass over the rows. The synthesis is its transpose with Ĝ(ℓ). A transform costs O(nlat·lmax²) for the recurrence and O(M·lmax) for the dense longitude sums, O(M log nlon) by FFT, over M cells. lmax = nothing takes spin_band_limit.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.SeparableFootprint — Type
SeparableFootprint{T}

Per-axis kernel weight tables (gx, gy, read through _sepw), the denominator, and the scratch buffers of the two-pass separable convolution.

The denominator is invrenorm for Deformable on a masked grid: the reciprocal kernel mass over active cells, the same two-pass convolution applied to the mask. Otherwise it is the outer product of Nx_profile and Ny_profile: ZeroFill counts the offsets in the domain and past a bounded edge, Deformable on a fully active grid only those in the domain. Each axis is truncated at rad on its own, so the window is a square. For the Gaussian it differs from the disk-truncated engines by weights below the truncation tolerance of kernel_radius.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.ZeroFill — Type
ZeroFill <: AbstractMaskStrategy

Masked cells and the domain's exterior are zero-velocity water (Aluie et al. 2018; Storer et al. 2022): they contribute nothing to the numerator, and the kernel keeps its full mass, the cells past a bounded edge included. The kernel is then the same everywhere, so filtering commutes with derivatives and conserves the integral over all space, which on a periodic grid is the grid (Aluie 2019; Grooms et al. 2021, eq. 7).

The filtered field is defined at every cell, a masked one too: within the kernel's reach of an active cell it is nonzero over land.

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering._banded_build_invden — Method
_banded_build_invden(grid, taps, nbands, strategy, periodic_x, periodic_y, exterior) -> invden

The reciprocal window mass, accumulated once per plan by running _banded_row_accumulate! over a mass field:

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.filter_field! — Method
filter_field!(out, field, grid, kernel, scale; mask_strategy = ZeroFill(), filter_plan = nothing,
              execution = AutoBackend(), method = RealSpace(), transform = AutoSpectralBackend())

Filter a field on a grid using kernel at characteristic full width scale (ℓ), writing the result to out (returned).

Keyword Arguments

  • mask_strategy::AbstractMaskStrategy=ZeroFill(): how masked cells and a bounded edge enter the filter. ZeroFill filters the field extended by zero over masked cells and past each bounded edge, normalized by the kernel's full mass over the lattice continued at its edge spacing, so filtering commutes with spatial derivatives, conserves the integral over all space, and defines the output at masked cells too; a uniform field falls toward a coast. Deformable normalizes over the active in-domain cells of each window and zeroes masked cells, so a constant is reproduced next to a boundary and the kernel changes shape there.
  • filter_plan::Union{Nothing,AbstractFilterPlan}=nothing: a prebuilt plan_filter result, reused across calls on the same grid, kernel and scale. When supplied, mask_strategy, execution, method and transform are ignored.
  • execution::AbstractExecutionBackend=AutoBackend(): execution backend (SerialBackend, ThreadedBackend, GPUBackend, …).
  • method, transform: as for plan_filter.

For spherical grids the longitude footprint wraps only when the grid is periodic (isperiodic); distances use the great-circle (Haversine) metric.

geom = CartesianGeometry()
grid = StructuredGrid(geom, 0.0:1000.0:99_000.0, 0.0:1000.0:99_000.0, mask)
out = zeros(100, 100)
filter_field!(out, field, grid, TopHatKernel(), 5000.0; mask_strategy = Deformable())

CoarseGrainingEnergyFluxes.Filtering.plan_filter — Method
plan_filter(grid, kernel, scale; mask_strategy = ZeroFill(), execution = AutoBackend(),
            method = RealSpace(), transform = AutoSpectralBackend(),
            cache_strategy = AutoCache(), cache_byte_budget = default_cache_byte_budget(),
            grid_plan = nothing, scratch = nothing) -> AbstractFilterPlan

A reusable filter plan for execution, applied by filter_apply!(out, field, plan): the real-space footprint, or under method = Spectral() the transform plan. A spectral plan also takes batch = nb for a trailing batch axis and, over a spherical node set, nufft, the NUFFT library NUFSHT runs; a keyword the method does not take raises an ArgumentError. grid_plan and scratch are the parts a sweep shares; see plan_filter_sweep.

A transform runs on 1 thread under SerialBackend() and on Threads.nthreads() under ThreadedBackend(), AutoBackend() and GPUBackend(), in the device's memory under the last. Under MPIBackend(inner) every rank holds the field and the output. A nonuniform transform (NUFFT, NUFSHT) gives each rank a disjoint share of the points: the analysis F_k = Σⱼ wⱼ cⱼ exp(−i k⋅xⱼ) is a sum over points, so the ranks' partial sums add to it exactly, and each rank evaluates the filtered series at its own points. An FFT or FastSphericalHarmonics transform runs whole on every rank. Under DistributedBackend(inner) the worker processes share the transform, a nonuniform one by blocks of points and an FFT by blocks of columns and then of rows, each at inner's thread count; a FastSphericalHarmonics transform runs in the calling process.
